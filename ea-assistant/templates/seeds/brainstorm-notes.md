## Two Layers of Intent — Brainstorm Prompt

When brainstorming, distinguish between:
- **Business layer** — what the business wants to do (capabilities, processes, customer journeys, revenue streams)
- **EA / TOGAF layer** — how we govern and enable change (architecture principles, standards, review boards, governance processes, reference architectures)

If a thought feels ambiguous, apply the quick test: *Would this still exist if the EA team were disbanded?*
- **Yes** → record it under `# Business Layer`
- **No** → record it under `# EA / TOGAF Layer`

See `skills/ea-artifact-templates/references/ea-concepts.md` → **Two Layers of Intent** for full guidance.
