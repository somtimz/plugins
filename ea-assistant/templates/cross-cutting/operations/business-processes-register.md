<details>
<summary>📋 Guidance</summary>

The Business Processes Register captures all structured, repeatable activity flows for this engagement.

A Business Process is the **organisational "how"** — a sequence of steps triggered by an event, consuming inputs and producing outputs, governed by business rules and enabled by capabilities and services. Processes answer *"how does the organisation perform the work?"* — distinct from Value Streams (*"what value is created for whom?"*) and Use Cases (*"what does an actor want to achieve?"*).

Capture every process with:
  - **Name** — verb-noun phrase (e.g. "Approve Supplier Invoice")
  - **Purpose** — why the process exists
  - **Owner** — accountable role
  - **Trigger / Inputs / Outputs**
  - **Steps** — activity, responsible role, system/tool, governing business rule
  - **Linked Value Streams** — VS-NNN IDs the process participates in
  - **Linked Capabilities** — CAP-NNN IDs the process exercises
  - **Linked Use Cases** — UC-NNN IDs that drive the process
  - **Linked Business Rules / Services** — BR-NNN / SVC-NNN IDs

**Traceability:**
  - Processes trace to Value Streams, Capabilities, Use Cases, Business Rules, and Business Services.
  - Use `/ea-processes trace` to verify linkages.

**Quality indicators:**
- Every process has an owner
- Steps are numbered and have responsible role and system/tool
- Active processes link to at least one value stream, capability, use case, rule, or service
- Business rules that govern process decisions are linked as BR-NNN
- Process inputs/outputs are named and testable

**Common mistakes:**
- Confusing a process with a value stream — a process is internal; a stream is stakeholder-to-outcome
- Confusing a process with a use case — a use case is actor-goal interaction; a process may support many use cases
- Vague triggers — "as needed" is not event-driven
- Missing owner — without ownership, process improvement stalls

**TOGAF reference:** TOGAF 10 §22 — Business Architecture. Process modelling supports capability mapping, service design, and gap analysis.

</details>
