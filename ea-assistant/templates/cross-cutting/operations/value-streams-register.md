<details>
<summary>📋 Guidance</summary>

The Value Streams Register captures all end-to-end value-creating flows for this engagement.

A Value Stream is a **cross-functional, stakeholder-to-outcome flow** composed of stages. Each stage adds value and is triggered by an event, performs activities, and produces an outcome. Value Streams answer *"what must happen for the stakeholder to receive value?"* — not *"how does the organisation perform the work?"* (that is a Business Process).

Capture every value stream with:
  - **Name** — outcome-oriented noun phrase (e.g. "Procure to Pay")
  - **Stakeholder** — who ultimately receives value
  - **Stages** — trigger → activities → outcome for each stage
  - **Linked Capabilities** — CAP-NNN IDs that enable the stream
  - **Linked Business Processes** — PROC-NNN IDs that operationalise the stream
  - **Trace to Goals** — G-NNN / OBJ-NNN IDs the stream realises

**Traceability:**
  - Value Streams trace to Capabilities (CAP-NNN) and Business Processes (PROC-NNN).
  - Use `/ea-valuestreams trace` to verify linkages.

**Quality indicators:**
- Every value stream has a named stakeholder
- Stages form a continuous trigger-to-outcome chain
- Each stage has a clear trigger, activities, and outcome
- Value streams link to at least one capability, process, or goal
- High-value streams are linked to business goals

**Common mistakes:**
- Confusing a value stream with a business process — a stream is end-to-end value; a process is internal "how"
- Too many stages — keep them at the stakeholder-outcome level (3–7 stages)
- Missing stakeholder — without one, value cannot be verified
- Missing trace to goals — orphan streams lack business justification

**TOGAF reference:** TOGAF 10 §22 — Business Architecture. Value streams are a core Business Architecture deliverable and input to capability and process modelling.

</details>
