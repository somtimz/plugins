<details>
<summary>📋 Guidance</summary>

The Business Services Register captures all externally visible units of behaviour offered to consumers through a defined contract.

Services exist at three levels:
  - **Business Service** — offered to customers or business units (e.g. "Claims Settlement")
  - **Application Service** — offered by an application (e.g. "Payment Authorisation")
  - **Technology Service** — offered by infrastructure (e.g. "Managed Database Hosting")

Capture every Business-level service with the **Service Passport**:
  - **Consumer** — who uses the service
  - **Outcome** — the value they receive
  - **Interface** — the channel through which it is consumed
  - **Owner** — accountable role
  - **SLA / NFR** — service level or requirement reference
  - **Linked Business Rules** — BR-NNN rules the service operationalises

**Traceability:**
  - Services trace to Capabilities (CAP-NNN) that realise them.
  - Services trace to ABB/SBB components that implement them.
  - Services trace to Interfaces (IFC-NNN) through which they are consumed.
  - Business-level services trace to Business Rules (BR-NNN) they enact.
  - Use `/ea-services trace` to verify service linkages.
  - Use `/ea-services coverage` to assess capability coverage.

**Quality indicators:**
- Every service has a clear Level
- Business-level services have Consumer, Outcome, and Interface populated
- Every active service links to at least one capability, ABB, SBB, or interface
- Business services that enact rules link to BR-NNN
- Interfaces are captured as IFC-NNN where they cross team, vendor, or trust boundaries

**Common mistakes:**
- Confusing a service with a business process — a process is the internal "how"; a service is the external "what"
- Confusing a service with a capability — a capability is the organisation's potential; a service is behaviour actually offered
- Capturing only Business services and ignoring Application/Technology services
- Missing interfaces — a service without an interface cannot be consumed

**TOGAF reference:** TOGAF 10 §22 (Business Architecture) and §24/§25 (Application/Technology Architecture). Service catalogues are maintained throughout the ADM cycle.

</details>
